# ℹ️ A little bit about me
Hi, I’m Sumant Shanbag. My journey has been shaped by two passions: technology and exploration. For more than 20 years, I’ve worked at the intersection of software architecture, security, and leadership—helping organizations simplify complex systems, build secure platforms, and guide teams to deliver with confidence. I’ve been fortunate to play roles that challenged me to solve problems in creative ways, whether it was modernizing legacy platforms, designing cloud-native solutions, or mentoring the next generation of engineers.

But my story doesn’t end at the keyboard. I’m also an avid hiker and badminton player, always seeking new challenges beyond work. Hiking has taken me from the rugged trails of the White Mountains—including summiting Mt. Washington—to unforgettable journeys like Everest Base Camp, Machu Picchu, Gray’s Peak in the Rockies, Mt. Katahdin, and the Grand Canyon’s South Rim. Each trail has reminded me of the value of preparation, persistence, and perspective—lessons that carry over into my professional life.

Whether I’m architecting software or tackling a mountain, I approach challenges with curiosity, resilience, and a drive to make the experience meaningful. For me, both work and play are about exploration—finding better paths, learning along the way, and enjoying the climb.
